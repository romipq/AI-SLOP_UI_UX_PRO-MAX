## Mixing your own

1. Product = `{AI} + {ordinary noun}`.
2. Audience = "modern teams", "builders", "founders", "creators".
3. Promise = `{10x} {outcome} in {seconds}`.
4. Sections = 9–14 from the anatomy.
5. Twist = one interaction that looks useful and isn't.
6. Level = LITE / CLASSIC / MAX / NUCLEAR.
7. Verify with `tools/slop_lint.py`.
