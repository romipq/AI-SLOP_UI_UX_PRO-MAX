Questions? We've got answers.

Is Nexora really free to start?

Absolutely! Our Starter plan is free forever — no credit card required. Upgrade to Pro anytime to unlock unlimited workflows and 50,000 AI credits per month. ✨

How is my data protected?

Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest (AES-256), and we're SOC 2 Type II certified. We never train models on your data — enterprise-grade security, by default. 🔒

Does Nexora integrate with my existing tools?

Yes! Nexora seamlessly connects with 200+ tools including Slack, Notion, GitHub, Figma, HubSpot and Zapier. If we don't support one yet, our API has you covered.

Can I cancel anytime?

Of course. Cancel in one click from your dashboard — no calls, no hoops, no awkward retention emails. You'll keep access until the end of your billing period.

Do I need technical skills to use Nexora?

Not at all. If you can describe what you want in plain English, Nexora can build it. Our AI-powered builder handles the rest — no code required. 🚀
