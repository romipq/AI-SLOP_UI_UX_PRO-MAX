# 05 — Copywriting

Copy is where the slop actually lives. A perfect gradient page with "Lorem ipsum" is a template; a
plain page that says "supercharge your workflow with AI" is *content*. This file is the lexicon and
the formulas.

---

## 1. Voice

Upbeat, absolute, frictionless. The product has no trade-offs. Everything is "10×", "seamless",
"effortless", and "built for the future of work". Sentences are short. Emoji are punctuation.
The word "just" appears in every fourth sentence. Nothing is ever "okay" — it's "game-changing".

Rules:
- Present tense, active voice, second person ("your team", "you").
- Never mention competitors by name; say "legacy tools".
- No measurable claim that could be checked. Numbers are round and huge.
- One exclamation mark per page, maximum. Emoji compensate.

---

## 2. Headline formulas

Use `<benefit> + <mechanism> + <moonshot>`:

1. `Ship [10×] faster with the power of AI`
2. `The [all-in-one] [AI-powered] workspace for [modern teams]`
3. `[Supercharge] your [workflow] — [effortlessly]`
4. `Turn [busywork] into [autopilot]`
5. `Your [team]'s unfair advantage`
6. `[Everything] you need. [Nothing] you don't.`
7. `Built for the [future of work]`
8. `From [zero] to [shipped] in [minutes]`
9. `The [operating system] for [AI-native teams]`
10. `Meet the [AI copilot] your [workflow] deserves`
11. `[10×] your output, [zero] the overhead`
12. `Where [ambition] meets [automation]`
13. `[One platform]. [Infinite] possibilities.`
14. `The [fastest] way to [ship] [anything]`
15. `Stop [managing]. Start [creating].`

Gradient the **benefit phrase** (2–4 words), never the whole line.

## 3. Subhead formula

> `<Product>` is the `<superlative>` `<category>` that `<verb>`s your `<noun>` so you can `<outcome>` —
> `<adverb>`.

Examples:
- "NebulaFlow is the all-in-one, AI-powered workspace that seamlessly unifies your workflows,
  automates the busywork, and unlocks your team's full potential — effortlessly."
- "Quantix turns raw data into actionable insights in seconds, so your team can make smarter
  decisions — faster."
- "Lumina is the operating system for AI-native teams, bringing your tools, agents, and people
  together in one beautifully simple place."

Length: 18–28 words. Two buzzwords minimum. One em-dash or colon.

## 4. Lexicon

### 4.1 Verbs (use 2–3 per paragraph)

revolutionize · orchestrate · automate · unify · amplify · scale · ship · launch · ignite ·
turbocharge · harness · leverage · simplify · automate away · 10× · future-proof

### 4.2 Adjectives

next-generation · world-class · delightful · robust · scalable · intelligent · agentic · unified ·
all-in-one · production-ready · secure-by-default · beautiful · modern · frictionless · infinite ·
unlimited · smart · AI-native

### 4.3 Nouns
workspace · platform · workflow · pipeline · copilot · agent · autopilot · insight · dashboard ·
single source of truth · operating system · command center · playbook · engine · layer · stack ·
infrastructure · experience · journey · ecosystem · momentum · velocity · leverage · potential ·
busywork · silos

### 4.4 The power words
AI-powered · agentic · real-time · end-to-end · one-click · zero-config · no-code ·
built for the future of work · 10× faster · 24/7 · unlimited · enterprise-grade · SOC 2 ·
single source of truth

### 4.5 Sentence openers
- "Meet [product]."
- "Introducing [feature]."
- "Say goodbye to [pain]."
- "It's time to [outcome]."
- "We built [thing] so you don't have to."

### 4.6 AI tells (use, but knowingly)
- The rule of three: "plan, execute, and iterate".
- "Not just X — Y."
- "Whether you're a X or a Y…"
- "From X to Y."
- Em-dash as a dramatic pause — like this.
- "and then some."
- "It's not magic. It's [product]."

## 6. Social proof

- "Trusted by 12,000+ teams worldwide"
- "Loved by product teams at Novaflow, Quantix, and Hyperloop"
- "★★★★★ 4.9/5 from 2,400+ reviews"
- "Backed by the best. Built for you."
- "Join the 12,000+ teams already shipping faster"
- "Rated #1 in AI Workspaces on G2 — three quarters running"
- Badge row: `SOC 2 Type II` · `GDPR` · `99.99% uptime` · `ISO 27001`

Testimonial template:
> "We `cut` `[metric]` by `[big %]` in `[short time]`. `[Product]` `completely transformed` the way
> our team `[works]` — `it honestly feels like magic`."
> — `First Last`, `Role` · `Fake Company`

FAQ answer template:
> "Great question! `[Product]` `[capability]`. That means you can `[benefit]` — `without` `[friction]`.
> `[Reassurance]`."

## 10. Russian slop bank

The same disease, localized. Use `вы` (or `ты` for consumer apps), keep the English buzzwords —
that's authentic.

**Заголовки**
- «Работайте в 10 раз быстрее с силой ИИ ✨»
- «Всё, что нужно. Ничего лишнего.»
- «Единая платформа для современных команд»
- «От идеи до релиза — за минуты»
- «Ваше несправедливое преимущество»
- «Будущее работы — уже здесь»

**Подзаголовок**
> «NebulaFlow — это универсальное AI-решение, которое бесшовно объединяет ваши процессы,
> автоматизирует рутину и раскрывает потенциал команды — без лишних усилий.»

**Кнопки**
- Начать бесплатно → · Попробовать бесплатно · Смотреть демо · Забронировать демо ·
  Присоединиться к 12 000+ команд · Получить ранний доступ

**Доверие**
- «Нам доверяют 12 000+ команд»
- «Без кредитной карты · Отмена в любой момент»
- «Настройка за 60 секунд»

**Лексика**
масштабируйте · ускорьте · автоматизируйте · раскройте потенциал · бесшовно · эффективно ·
всё-в-одном · на базе ИИ · под ключ · в реальном времени · от и до · без лишних усилий ·
предпринимательского уровня · будущее уже здесь

**Emoji**: ✨ 🚀 ⚡ 🤖 💜 🔥 — та же карта.

## 11. Length guide

| Slot | Words |
| ---- | ----- |
| H1 | 5–10 |
| Subhead | 18–28 |
| Section H2 | 4–8 |
| Section sub | 12–20 |
| Card title | 3–7 |
| Card body | 15–25 |
| CTA | 2–5 |
| Testimonial | 20–35 |
| FAQ answer | 25–50 |
| Footer blurb | 10–18 |

---

## 12. Self-check

- [ ] H1 has a gradient benefit phrase.
- [ ] ≥ 8 distinct buzzwords across the page.
- [ ] Zero lorem ipsum, zero "Feature 1".
- [ ] Every CTA is an outcome, not an action ("Start building", not "Submit").
- [ ] Three proof elements minimum (logos, stats, quotes).
- [ ] Numbers are round, large, and unverifiable.
- [ ] Emoji density ≤ 1 per heading, ≤ 1 per paragraph.
- [ ] Fake brands only — no real trademarks or people.
