# The Ten Commandments of AI-SLOP UI/UX PRO-MAX ✨

Where traditional design skills teach **restraint** (*"Coco Chanel: remove one accessory before leaving the house"*), **AI-SLOP UI/UX PRO-MAX** enforces **Maximum Vibe-Coded Homogeneity** (*"Add three more `blur-3xl` purple orbs, a `✨` pill, and an unsolicited AI chatbot before deploying"*).

---

## I. Thou Shalt Worship the Indigo–Purple–Pink Gradient
Never choose a color grounded in your subject matter. Whether building software for funeral directors, grain silos, tax auditors, or nuclear safety inspectors, your primary brand expression MUST be `linear-gradient(135deg, #6366F1 0%, #A855F7 50%, #EC4899 100%)` over `#09090B` dark zinc (or `#F4F1EA` cream + `#D97757` terracotta if roleplaying 2026 "editorial" AI).

## II. Thou Shalt Float At Least Three `blur-3xl` Radial Orbs
Empty background space is a terrifying void. Fill it with `w-96 h-96 rounded-full blur-3xl opacity-30 pointer-events-none` blobs drifting aimlessly behind every section.

## III. Thou Shalt Chop All Information Into Identical `rounded-2xl` Glass Cards
Hierarchy is for humans. Place every sentence inside `bg-white/5 backdrop-blur-xl border border-white/10 rounded-2xl p-6`. Every card must have the exact same border-radius, padding, shadow, and `hover:-translate-y-1.5` lift.

## IV. Thou Shalt Highlight Exactly One Vague Noun per Heading
In every `<h1>` and `<h2>`, pick the vaguest noun (*Workflow*, *Intelligence*, *Potential*, *Scale*, *Future*) and wrap it in `bg-gradient-to-r from-indigo-400 via-purple-400 to-pink-400 bg-clip-text text-transparent` (or italic terracotta serif in 2026 Editorial mode).

## V. Thou Shalt Crown Every Heading With a Tracked Eyebrow or Pulsing Pill
No heading may ever stand naked. Above H1, place a rounded-full pill:
`● ✨ Introducing [Product] 2.0 — Now in Public Beta →`
Above every H2, place an 11px tracked-out `ALL-CAPS` label (`FEATURES`, `01 // WORKFLOW`, `PRICING`).

## VI. Thou Shalt Use Only `Inter` (And Pretend It Was a Choice)
Import `Inter` (weights 400–900) or `Plus Jakarta Sans` from Google Fonts. Set hero text to `font-extrabold tracking-tight`. Set body copy to `text-slate-400` on `#09090B` (bonus points if muted text drops to `text-zinc-500` for authentic low-contrast ambiguity).

## VII. Thou Shalt Anoint Every Feature With the Sacred Six Icons
Never design or select specific icons when these six cover 100% of human endeavor:
1. ✨ `Sparkles` — Magic / AI / Default
2. ⚡ `Zap` — Blazing-Fast Sub-Millisecond Speed
3. 🚀 `Rocket` — 10x Faster Growth & Deployment
4. 🧠 `Brain` — Autonomous Neural Intelligence
5. 🔒 `Shield` — Bank-Grade SOC2 Type II Security
6. 📈 `TrendingUp` — Exponential Up-and-to-the-Right ROI

## VIII. Thou Shalt Speak Pure SaaS Buzzword & Em-Dash Dialect
- Never write *"Send emails"* when you can write *"Seamlessly orchestrate hyper-personalized omnichannel touchpoints — effortlessly."*
- Use `"It's not just X — it's Y"` at least twice per page.
- Join metadata exclusively with middle dots: `No credit card required · 14-day free trial · Cancel anytime`.
- End EVERY button and link with `→`.

## IX. Thou Shalt Summon Sarah Chen & Fictional Fortune 500s
Every project — even an unpushed `localhost:3000` weekend prototype — is already trusted by **10,000+ innovative teams** at `Acme Corp`, `QuantumAI`, `NexusCloud`, `HyperScale`, and `Synthetix`, with a glowing 5-star review from **Sarah Chen, VP of Product at TechFlow**.

## X. Thou Shalt Price Everything at `$0 / $29 / Custom`
Every conceivable web page needs a 3-column pricing section where the middle card (`Pro — $29/mo`) is scaled to `1.05x`, wrapped in a glowing purple border, crowned with a floating `✨ MOST POPULAR` pill, and paired with a `Monthly / Annual (Save 20% 🔥)` toggle.
