# Field Guide to All Seven Eras of AI-Generated UI Slop (2023–2026)

Pass `--era <era>` to `slop.py` to lock onto a specific evolutionary branch of LLM aesthetic monoculture!

---

## 1. `2024-nebula` — Classic v0 / Bolt Indigo-Purple Nebula
- **Canvas**: `#09090B` or `#030712` with 1px `white/[0.04]` grid.
- **Signature**: `from-indigo-500 via-purple-500 to-pink-500`, 3–6 floating `blur-3xl` radial orbs, `bg-white/5 backdrop-blur-xl border-white/10 rounded-2xl` frosted glass cards, `Inter` 800-weight headings, glowing purple `$29/mo` Pro tier.
- **Best For**: Peak instant recognizability — unmistakable from 20 paces away.

## 2. `2025-bento` — Lovable Daylight Mesh & Decorative Bento Maximalism
- **Canvas**: `#F8FAFC` with blurred pastel lavender/sky/rose blobs OR `#0B0F19` dot-matrix bento.
- **Signature**: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, `rounded-3xl` pillow cards, asymmetric 6-column Bento grids stuffed with decorative widgets (orbiting Slack/Notion/Linear icons, dummy `⌘K` keycaps, `99.8%` radial gauges).
- **Best For**: Friendly B2B SaaS, Product Hunt launches, HR, EdTech, Telehealth.

## 3. `2026-editorial` — Claude Warm-Cream & Terracotta Serif ("Neo-Slop")
- **Canvas**: Unbleached warm cream `#F4F1EA` with `#E2DDD3` 1px hairline rules.
- **Signature**: Emerged when prompts banned purple gradients, causing frontier models to converge on a brand-new cliché: `Instrument Serif` or `Newsreader` headlines where **every word is Roman except ONE emotional word italicized in warm terracotta `#D97757`**, tracked-out `11px ALL-CAPS` eyebrows (`01 // THE MANIFESTO`), middle-dot metadata (`CRAFT · VELOCITY · AUTONOMY`), and `→` arrows.
- **Best For**: Design studios, AI research labs, stealth startups roleplaying artisanal restraint.

## 4. `cyber-acid` — Terminal Brutalist Acid-Lime Slop
- **Canvas**: Pitch `#0A0A0A` with exposed `1px #1F1F1F` structural grid lines and `+` corner crosshairs.
- **Signature**: `0px` sharp border-radius everywhere, `JetBrains Mono` uppercase typography, `[ 01 // SYSTEM_ARMED ]` bracketed eyebrows, blinking `_` cursor on H1, and `#CCFF00` neon acid-lime buttons.
- **Best For**: Autonomous agent runtimes, Rust CLIs, defense-tech, cybersecurity, zero-knowledge infra.

## 5. `shadcn-default` — Untouched Zinc-950 Boilerplate
- **Canvas**: Solid `#09090B` (`zinc-950`) with `1px solid #27272A` (`zinc-800`) borders and `--radius: 0.5rem`.
- **Signature**: Zero tokens changed from `npx shadcn@latest init`: 4 top KPI cards (`$45,231.89 · +20.1% from last month`), `Overview | Analytics | Reports` tabs, `Olivia Martin (+$1,999.00)` in Recent Sales, and split-screen auth with Sofia Davis's quote.
- **Best For**: SaaS admin portals, CRUD apps, Next.js boilerplates.

## 6. `ai-wrapper` — Centered Conic Prompt Box & Agentic Node Graphs
- **Canvas**: `#0D0D12` with prismatic rainbow-purple conic border beams.
- **Signature**: `"✨ What can I help you build today?"` centered over a glowing textarea + 4 emoji starter pills (`🚀 Build a SaaS`, `📊 Analyze CSV`), OR a decorative node-graph canvas connecting `[Webhook] ──✨──> [LLM Router] ──✨──> [Slack]`.
- **Best For**: Chatbots, RAG PDF wrappers, AI IDEs, multimodal studios.

## 7. `web3-hype` — Neon Vaporwave APY & Obsidian Mastermind Funnels
- **Canvas**: `#060314` with magenta `#D946EF` + cyan `#06B6D4` nebulae (or `#080808` + metallic gold `#F59E0B`).
- **Signature**: `"Connect Wallet →"`, `$4.2B+ TVL`, `42,069% APY`, Solana `#9945FF -> #14F195` gradients, or VSL funnel pages with strikethrough `$2,997 -> $99`.
- **Best For**: DeFi protocols, memecoin launchpads, AI-agency coaching funnels.
