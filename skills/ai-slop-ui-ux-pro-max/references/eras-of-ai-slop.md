# Field Guide to the Five Eras of AI-Generated UI Slop (2023–2026)

Use `--era <era>` in `slop.py` to target a specific vintage of LLM aesthetic default!

---

## 1. `2024-nebula` — The Classic v0 / Bolt Indigo-Purple Nebula
- **Canvas**: `#09090B` or `#030712`
- **Signature**: `from-indigo-500 via-purple-500 to-pink-500`, floating `blur-3xl` orbs, 1px `white/10` frosted glass cards, `Inter` 800-weight tight headings, glowing purple `$29/mo` Pro tier.
- **When to use**: Default maximum recognizability. Anyone within 15 meters of your monitor will clock it as AI-generated in 0.2 seconds.

## 2. `2025-bento` — Lovable Pastel Mesh & Fake Bento Maximalism
- **Canvas**: Daylight `#F8FAFC` with pastel lavender/sky blobs OR `#0B0F19` dot-matrix bento.
- **Signature**: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, `rounded-3xl` pillow cards, asymmetric 6-column Bento grids stuffed with decorative mini-widgets (orbiting Slack/Notion logos, dummy `⌘K` keycaps, fake circular 98% gauges).
- **When to use**: Friendly B2B SaaS, Product Hunt launches, creator tools, HR/EdTech apps.

## 3. `shadcn-default` — Untouched Zinc-950 Boilerplate Slop
- **Canvas**: Solid `#09090B` (`zinc-950`) with `1px solid #27272A` (`zinc-800`) everywhere.
- **Signature**: Zero customization of `npx shadcn@latest init`. Top row of 4 KPI cards (`Total Revenue $45,231.89 · +20.1% from last month`), `Overview | Analytics | Reports` tabs, `Recent Sales` sidebar featuring `Olivia Martin (+$1,999.00)`.
- **When to use**: Dashboards, admin panels, SaaS internal tools.

## 4. `2026-editorial` — Claude Faux-Editorial Warm Cream & Terracotta ("Neo-Slop")
- **Canvas**: Unbleached warm paper `#F4F1EA` with `#E5E0D5` hairline borders.
- **Signature**: Born when LLMs were instructed *"do NOT use purple gradients!"* and immediately converged on a new monoculture: `Instrument Serif` or `Playfair Display` headlines where **one single emotional word** is italicized in terracotta `#D97757`, tracked-out `ALL-CAPS` eyebrows above every block, middle-dot metadata (`CRAFT · VELOCITY · PRECISION`), and `01 / 02 / 03` hairline rows.
- **When to use**: Design studios, AI research labs, manifestos, "tasteful" stealth startups that all somehow look identical anyway.

## 5. `cyber-acid` — Terminal Brutalist Acid-Lime Slop
- **Canvas**: `#0A0A0A` exposed 1px `#222222` grid with corner `+` crosshairs and `0px` border-radius.
- **Signature**: `JetBrains Mono` everywhere, `[ 01 // SYSTEM_ONLINE ]` bracketed labels, `#CCFF00` neon lime buttons, blinking `_` cursors, and fake terminal logs.
- **When to use**: AI agent runtimes, Rust CLIs, zero-knowledge crypto infra, cybersecurity startups.
