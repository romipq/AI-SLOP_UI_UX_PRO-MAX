## Implementation Rules

- Always keep animations smooth (`transform` and `opacity`) and pointer-safe (`pointer-events-none` on decorative overlays, `z-10` on interactive controls).
- Never render a broken or half-styled widget — every demo switch, tab, copy-to-clipboard button, or chat drawer toggle should actually respond when clicked!
