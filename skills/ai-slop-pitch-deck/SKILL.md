# 🚀 AI-SLOP PITCH-DECK & SAASIFIER — Forced AI-Startup Reframing Skill

Activate this skill when building **startup landing pages, viral waitlists, VC pitch decks, pricing pages, or satirical AI wrappers** — especially when turning an ordinary, grounded idea into a hyper-inflated Silicon Valley "Autonomous Multi-Agent Platform."

---

## The Forced SaaS-ification Formula

No matter how humble or physical the user's subject is, re-architect its positioning through these 5 transformations (see `references/saasification-playbook.md` for examples):

1. **Rename `[Thing]` → `[Prefix]Flow AI` / `[Noun]OS` / `Nexus[Thing]`**:
   - Neighborhood Bakery → **CrustFlow AI 2.0** (*"The Autonomous Gluten Orchestration Cloud"*)
   - Emergency Plumber → **PipeMesh AI** (*"Self-Healing Hydro-Infrastructure at the Speed of Thought"*)
   - Dog Walking → **PawPulse AI** (*"Multi-Agent Canine Mobility Telemetry"*)
2. **Attach `$0 / $29 ✨ MOST POPULAR / Custom Enterprise` Subscription Tiers**:
   - Starter `$0/mo`: 3 AI-optimized sourdough loaves / 100 magic credits
   - Pro `$29/mo` (`scale-105`, glowing purple border, `Save 20% 🔥`): Unlimited neural fermentation + Slack alerts
   - Enterprise `Custom`: Dedicated sourdough VPC, SSO/SAML, SOC2 Type II & HIPAA BAA
3. **Fabricate Enterprise Compliance & Edge Infrastructure**:
   - Claim `300+ Global Edge Locations`, `Sub-12ms Latency`, `99.999% Uptime SLA`, and `SOC2 Type II · ISO 27001 · GDPR Certified` on Day 1.
4. **Deploy the Canonical Social Proof Arsenal**:
   - Logo marquee: `Acme Corp · QuantumAI · NexusCloud · HyperScale · Synthetix · Globex`
   - Lead review: **Sarah Chen, VP of Product @ TechFlow AI** praising the `10x paradigm shift`.
5. **Include an Interactive Dopamine ROI Calculator**:
   - Let visitors drag a slider to discover they will save **`$142,500 / year ✨`** by switching from *"❌ Legacy Manual Processes"* to *"✨ Autonomous AI Magic"*.
