❓ FAQ

Reasonable questions about unreasonable design

Is this a joke?

Both. The output is a parody, but the tooling is real: a working skill, a weighted linter, a design system, and examples that actually run. The joke is that the clichés are predictable enough to be engineered.

Should I use this for a real product?

Only if the product is a parody, a teaching tool, or a benchmark. The examples intentionally break accessibility rules, ignore performance, and use fictional social proof. Scroll up: there's a whole category called “A11Y sins.”

Why a linter instead of just a prompt?

Because “more gradients” is not a measurable instruction. SLOP-100 turns the vibe into 90 weighted signals, so an agent can verify its own output, CI can gate it, and two pages can be compared without arguing.

Does it work in languages other than English?

Yes. There's a Russian/Ukrainian buzzword bank (“бесшовная интеграция”, “масштабируйте”, “инновационные решения”), and the copy formulas are language-agnostic. The gradients translate perfectly.

Can I use it to evaluate real UIs?

Absolutely — that's the “roast” mode. Point the linter at a production page, get a score and receipts. Anything above 80 is a page that could have been generated in one prompt, which is the actual critique.
